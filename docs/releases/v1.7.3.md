# ModSync 1.7.3

## Confirm and move ModSync outside your instance

- Copies opened inside a BigChadGuys instance now ask you to confirm the UltimMC folder or choose another one.
- Creates `UltimMC\ModSync` after confirmation when missing.
- Verifies the copied executable and waits for the new copy to confirm startup before removing the old executable.
- Offers to open an existing destination copy without overwriting it. Cancellation and failed relocation preserve the original.
- Supports locations beneath hidden Windows folders. Game files, existing launch commands, and other old files stay in place.

After moving, confirm your instance and personal-mod exclusions, update old shortcuts, and review **Launch check** setup with UltimMC closed. Matching legacy settings can be imported during instance selection. Launching through an old pre-launch command cancels that launch and asks you to open ModSync directly first.

Validation: 141 automated tests, including hidden-parent folders, missing-folder creation, destination conflicts, corrupted copies, changed originals, and light/dark layouts. Real player instances were not modified during development or testing.

The v1.7.2 workflow stopped before publication after detecting the hidden-folder issue; this release includes the fix.
